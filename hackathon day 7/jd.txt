Job Overview

Reliance Industries Limited is seeking a Junior Application Developer to join our dynamic team at our Navi Mumbai location. This is a full-time position where you will be responsible for developing, enhancing, and maintaining software applications to meet the companys strategic objectives. The ideal candidate will have a passion for technology and a desire to innovate while ensuring the highest levels of quality in their work.

Qualifications And Skills

Strong problem-solving skills (Mandatory skill)
In-depth knowledge of the software development lifecycle (Mandatory skill)
Experience in database management (Mandatory skill)
Proficiency in one or more programming languages
Expertise in web development and related technologies
Understanding of version control systems such as Git
Experience working within an Agile methodology
Strong testing and debugging capabilities

Roles And Responsibilities

Develop, enhance, and maintain software applications based on business requirements
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define, design, and ship new features
Conduct code reviews and ensure the highest levels of code quality and performance
Troubleshoot and resolve software defects and issues reported by users
Participate in Agile development processes, including daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
Maintain documentation for software development activities and processes
Stay updated with the latest trends and technologies in software development to bring new ideas and innovations to the team
Ensure compliance with industry standards and best practices in software development
